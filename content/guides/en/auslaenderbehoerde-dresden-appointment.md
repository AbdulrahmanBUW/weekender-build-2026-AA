## Before you go
The Ausländerbehörde (foreigners office) in Dresden only sees people with an appointment. There are no fixed opening hours for general visitors (Öffnungszeiten: nach Vereinbarung, by appointment only). Before every visit, contact the office first and book an appointment if needed.

You can book an appointment in three ways:
- Online booking
- By **email**: auslaenderbehoerde@dresden.de
- By **phone hotline**: 0351 488 6009 (Tuesday and Thursday 9–12 and 13–16, Friday 9–12)

Waiting times for appointments can be long because there are many requests and little staff. Plan early.

Emergency appointments are only possible in special cases, for example if your Aufenthaltstitel (residence permit) runs out in less than 2 weeks, or in a medical emergency. A private or work trip is not a reason for an emergency appointment.

**Note:** The official visitor address is Lingnerallee 3, **Eingang Nord** (north entrance). One notice on dresden.de still says "Eingang A" for the same building.

## What to bring
- Your invitation letter (Einladungsschreiben) — it tells you which documents you need
- All documents named in your invitation letter

When you contact the office, tell them your name, first name, date of birth, and the reason for your request. It is best to contact them in German.

## At the appointment
1. **Be on time** and bring all required documents.
2. **Scan the QR code** from your invitation letter at the entrance. The screen will show which floor to go to.
3. **Biometric photo:** Only if your invitation letter says so. Photo stations are in the waiting area on the ground floor. Use your date of birth as the password.
4. **Go to the waiting area** on the floor shown in your invitation letter. If you only need to pick up a document, wait on the ground floor.
5. **Wait for your number.** When it is called, the screen shows your number and the room number. Then go to that room with your documents.

## Good to know
The office moved in May 2026. All appointments now take place at **Lingnerallee 3, Eingang Nord, 01069 Dresden**. The closest stops are Deutsches Hygiene-Museum and Pirnaischer Platz. At the time of writing, there is no barrier-free access at this location because of a change in the security concept.

Do not hand in documents at the security desk at the entrance: security staff may not accept documents for the Ausländerbehörde. There is no letterbox for the office at the new location. Use the city letterbox at the Rathaus Dresden or send your documents by post (Postfach 12 00 20, 01001 Dresden).
