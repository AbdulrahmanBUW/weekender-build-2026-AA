## Before you start
- In Dresden you apply online in the **Kita-Portal Dresden** for a place in a Kinderkrippe (crèche, under 3), a Kindergarten (from 3 until school) or in Kindertagespflege (a Tagesmutter or Tagesvater, a childminder who looks after up to five children). A place in a city-run Hort (after-school club) is not booked in the portal: you register directly with the Hort management at the school (or at the Kita with Hort places) before your child starts school.
- Since August 2013, children from their **first birthday** have a legal right (Rechtsanspruch) to a place in a Kita or with a childminder. Younger children can also have a right in some cases, for example when both parents work, look for work or are in training.
- You can apply **from birth**. The city recommends applying about **eleven months** before the start date you want. Places are given all year round.
- Places go first to families whose Hauptwohnsitz (main residence) is in Dresden. If you are moving to Dresden, apply as early as possible and write your moving date (and your new address, if you know it) in the Bemerkungsfeld (comments box). You get the place on condition that you and your child are registered in Dresden when care starts.

## How to apply
1. Search for Kitas and childminders in the Kita-Portal or on the city map (Themenstadtplan). Many Kitas have open days or information evenings for parents.
2. Fill in the online application. You can name up to **five** Wunscheinrichtungen (preferred Kitas or childminders), in your order of preference.
3. Write all first names of your child exactly as on the Geburtsurkunde (birth certificate). Your address must match your ID card; abbreviations or differences slow down the process.
4. Make a separate application for each child. You can mention a sibling in the comments box.
5. No internet at home? The Zentrale Beratungs- und Vermittlungsstelle (central advice and placement office) helps you in person, and the Städtische Bibliotheken (city libraries) have public computers you can use to apply.

## How places are offered (waiting list)
- Your first-choice Kita sees your application soon after you send it. **Eleven months** before your start date it can answer in the system.
- Each Kita has **14 days** to answer. If it does not decide or has no place, the next Kita on your list is asked, step by step every two weeks.
- You get an email about every step. When a Kita or childminder can offer a place, they contact you.
- If none of your Kitas can offer a place yet, you get a "zweite Zwischeninformation" (second interim notice). This is **not a rejection**: from now on your child is on the Warteliste (waiting list) of all Kitas in your application, until you sign a contract.
- If you still have no offer about **4 months** before your start date, the central office looks for an alternative (Kitas of all providers and childminders). You can also contact them yourself.
- When you accept a place, you sign a Betreuungsvertrag (care contract) with the Kita or childminder. In it you choose the daily care hours.

## Costs
- You pay a monthly Elternbeitrag (parent fee). In Dresden it is the same for city Kitas, Kitas of non-profit providers and childminders; it depends only on the type of care and the daily hours in your contract. Private Kitas set their own fees, usually higher.
- Example from 1 September 2026: a **9-hour Krippe place** (under 3) for the first child of a married couple or a couple living together costs **280.19 EUR per month**; a **9-hour Kindergarten place** costs **231.40 EUR per month** (source: dresden.de, Elternbeiträge, retrieved 26 Sep 2026). See the full table on dresden.de.
- The fee is lower for Alleinerziehende (single parents) and for the second child in care at the same time. From the third child in care at the same time, care is free (beitragsfrei).
- You pay **no fee** if you or your child receive Bürgergeld, Sozialhilfe (social assistance), Asylbewerberleistungen (asylum seeker benefits), Kinderzuschlag (child supplement) or Wohngeld (housing benefit). With a low income you can apply for a reduction.
- Apply at the Beitragsstelle (fee office) of the Amt für Kindertagesbetreuung, at the earliest **6 weeks** before care starts. Bring the care contract and your proof (for example the benefit decision). A reduction is valid for a limited time, so apply again in good time.
- Meals cost extra (Essengeld). Usually you make a contract with the meal provider. Families with a low income can get help with lunch costs through the Bildungspaket (education and participation package).

## Before the first day
- You must show that a doctor has examined your child: an ärztliche Bescheinigung (doctor's certificate, normally not older than 14 days) **or** the gelbes Heft (yellow child check-up booklet).
- You must also show that your child has had the publicly recommended vaccinations for its age (or declare that you do not agree to certain vaccinations), and proof of measles protection (Masernschutz). The city has a form for both.

## Help in your language
- The city has an information leaflet for immigrant parents in English, Russian, Ukrainian, Spanish and French (see the sources).
- Kitas, childminders and the Amt für Kindertagesbetreuung can bring in a Gemeindedolmetscher (community interpreter) for talks with parents; the costs are covered.
