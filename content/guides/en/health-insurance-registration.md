## Who must be insured?
- Health insurance is compulsory in Germany. All people who live in Germany must have health insurance.
- There are two systems: **statutory health insurance** (gesetzliche Krankenversicherung, GKV) and **private health insurance** (private Krankenversicherung, PKV). Most people (almost 90 percent) are in the statutory system. There are about 93 statutory funds (Krankenkassen).
- People who cannot get statutory insurance must take out private insurance.
- Statutory members are also insured in long-term care insurance (Pflegeversicherung).

## GKV or PKV?
- **Employees** are normally insured in the GKV through their job. In 2026, employees with a yearly salary above **77,400 EUR** (Versicherungspflichtgrenze, compulsory insurance limit) do not have to be in the GKV and can choose private insurance (source: Federal Ministry of Health, as of 3 Sept 2026).
- Self-employed people and civil servants (Beamte) can choose private insurance whatever their income. Private premiums depend on age, health and the chosen benefits.
- Moving from private back to statutory insurance is only possible in some situations. Think carefully before you choose PKV.

## How to register
### Employees
1. Choose a Krankenkasse. You are free to join any statutory fund.
2. Apply before or when you start work. Many funds have an online application (some in English).
3. Your insurer confirms your membership to your employer electronically. If someone asks for paper proof, request a **Mitgliedsbescheinigung** (membership certificate).

### Students (TU Dresden)
1. You must prove your insurance status before enrolment. Enrolment is refused without it.
2. **Statutory insurance:** tell your Krankenkasse that you will study at TU Dresden. The insurer sends your data electronically to the university. If needed, give them TU Dresden's sender number **HASN H0002752** (company number 05083454).
3. **Private insurance:** ask a statutory Krankenkasse for an exemption (Befreiung von der Versicherungspflicht) and ask it to report this to TU Dresden.
4. Compulsory student insurance applies until the age of 30. If you are older, still contact a statutory Krankenkasse; they will advise you.
5. Without the insurer's notification you get **no Campuscard** (student ID) and your enrolment cannot be completed.
6. Travel health insurance is not accepted.

### Spouses and children (family reunion)
- The embassy or Auslaenderbehoerde (foreigners office) asks for proof of health insurance for all family members. Statutory or private insurance can be used.
- If you have statutory insurance, your spouse or registered partner and your children may be insured **free of charge** through **Familienversicherung** (family insurance). In 2026 their regular total income must not be more than **565 EUR per month** (603 EUR for a mini-job) (source: TK, 2026).
- Children are normally family-insured until 18, until 23 if not working, and until 25 if they are in school, unpaid training or studying.
- Apply for family insurance with your Krankenkasse (online portal or form).
- Parents or parents-in-law of skilled workers (residence permit first issued on or after 1 March 2024) must pay for sufficient health and care insurance with their own money (source: BAMF).

## Mitgliedsbescheinigung (membership certificate)
- An official paper from your Krankenkasse that shows you are a member. It shows your name, date of birth, insurance number, start date and type of insurance (compulsory, family or voluntary).
- Employers, universities and authorities may ask for it. Often the insurer confirms your membership digitally, so you do not need paper.
- Request it online (member portal or app), by phone or by post.

## eGK (elektronische Gesundheitskarte, health insurance card)
- Your insurer sends the card by post. You must upload a passport photo first; the card can take up to four weeks.
- Show it at every doctor, hospital or therapist visit.
- The back of the card is also your European Health Insurance Card (EHIC) for other EU countries.
- If the card is not there yet, ask your insurer for a replacement confirmation for the doctor.

## Coming from the EU
- With the EHIC from your home country you can get necessary care during a temporary stay. If you move to Germany, ask your home insurer if you can get an **S1 form**.
- EU students with an EHIC still register with a statutory Krankenkasse in Germany, which reports to TU Dresden.

## What does it cost? (general)
- Statutory contributions depend on income, but benefits are the same for everyone.
- **General contribution rate** (allgemeiner Beitragssatz): **14.6 %** of income; reduced rate 14.0 %. **Average additional contribution** (durchschnittlicher Zusatzbeitragssatz) for 2026: **2.9 %**. Each fund sets its own additional contribution.
- Employees and employers each pay **half**. Contributions are counted on income up to **5,812.50 EUR per month** (Beitragsbemessungsgrenze, 2026).
- Source: Federal Ministry of Health, Beitraege page, as of 3 Sept 2026. Student contributions are different: ask your Krankenkasse.
- Family insurance (Familienversicherung) costs nothing extra.

## Before you arrive
- Choose your Krankenkasse (or private insurer) early. You need proof of insurance for the visa, the job start and university enrolment.
- Students: tell your insurer about TU Dresden before enrolment.
- Families: plan insurance for every family member before the visa appointment.
