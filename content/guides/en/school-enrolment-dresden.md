## Who must be registered?
- In Saxony every child must be registered at a Grundschule (primary school) in the year before school starts (Schulanmeldepflicht, compulsory registration). There is no exemption.
- For the school year **2027/2028**: children born between **1 July 2020 and 30 June 2021** must be registered. Children born between 1 July and 30 September 2021 **can** be registered (with the registration they become obliged to attend school). Children born later can start early only if the head teacher agrees after a school doctor's check.
- If you think your child is not ready yet, you still register it. After the school-entry check, the head teacher can decide on a Rückstellung (one-year deferral). This is possible only once.
- If you do not register your child, you can be fined.

## Where?
- The Amt für Schulen (schools office) sends a letter to the address where your child has its Hauptwohnsitz (main residence).
- Register at **one** municipal Grundschule in your Schulbezirk (school district), or at the Universitätsschule (open to all children who live in Dresden). Find your district in the **Grundschulfinder** on the city map.
- On the form you can name other schools in your district. There is no right to a place at one particular school. If your school is full, your child gets a place at another Grundschule in the district; you do not need to register again. Usually the distance to the school and siblings already at the school decide.
- You can also apply to private schools (Schulen in freier Trägerschaft), as many as you like. Then inform a school in your district with the form "Mitteilung Anmeldung" from dresden.de.
- Want a school outside your district? Register in your district first and hand in an Antrag auf Einschulung außerhalb des Schulbezirkes (application for a school outside the district) at the other school.

## When and how?
- **Online:** for 2027/2028 from 1 August to 15 September 2026 via schule.sachsen.de/gs-anmeldung. You need a **BundID** account (id.bund.de). Ask the school how it wants to meet you and which original documents to show.
- **In person:** for 2027/2028 the registration days were **Thursday 10 September and Tuesday 15 September 2026, 14:00–18:00**, directly at the school, no appointment needed. Some schools offer extra days. Places are not given out on the day, so it does not matter which day you come.
- **Missed it?** Register without delay: contact a Grundschule in your district and make an appointment.
- One parent is enough. Another person can register for you with a Vollmacht (power of attorney) signed by all parents with custody.
- Some schools want your child to come to the registration, others do not. The list is on dresden.de.

## What to bring
- Personalausweis (ID card) of the parent who registers (no German ID card? ask the school which document to bring)
- Geburtsurkunde (birth certificate) of the child
- The Anmeldebogen (registration form), filled in and signed (download on dresden.de)
- If needed: proof of alleiniges Sorgerecht (sole custody: court decision or confirmation from the Jugendamt, youth welfare office)
- If you are moving: rental contract signed by all parties, or Meldebescheinigung (registration certificate)

## School-entry medical check (Schulaufnahmeuntersuchung)
- Every child has this free, compulsory check with the Kinder- und Jugendärztlicher Dienst (children's medical service) of the Gesundheitsamt (health office). Checks take place from August to January and last about 40 minutes. Your child and at least one parent must come.
- Book the appointment online via dresden.de (the booking portal is open until 30 September 2026), later by phone with the service responsible for your school.
- After booking online you get an email: click the confirmation link within **12 hours**, otherwise the appointment is not fixed.
- Bring: the filled and signed Elternfragebogen (parent questionnaire, also available in simple German), Impfausweis (vaccination card), gelbes Heft (yellow check-up booklet), and doctor's, therapy or development reports if you have them.
- The doctor checks sight, hearing, height, weight and development in a playful way and looks at the vaccinations. Measles protection (Masernschutz) is asked for at this check, not at the school registration.
- Multilingual children can take an extra, free and voluntary language screening (LiSe-DaZ). Fill in the extra handout and bring it.
- Information sheets about the check exist in English, Arabic, Farsi, Dari, Turkish, Kurdish, Ukrainian and Russian (on the dresden.de page).

## After registration
- The head teacher decides on admission. You get the Aufnahmebescheid (admission letter) at the end of May / beginning of June.
- The Hort (after-school care) is **not** included in the school registration: register for it separately. For a city-run Hort you register directly with the Hort management at the school (or at the Kita with Hort places) before your child starts school.
- The Schuleinführung (school start celebration) is on the Saturday before the school year starts.
- Families with little money can get the Bildungspaket (education package): school supplies, lunch, tutoring, hobbies, trips and bus tickets.

## Children arriving from abroad at school age
- All children who live in Saxony must go to school, also the children of foreign citizens.
- Parents of school-age children whose first language is not German must first register them for the Besondere Bildungsberatung (special education counselling) at the Landesamt für Schule und Bildung, Dresden office (Großenhainer Straße 92, phone 0351 84390), before the children start school. Bring your ID or passport, your child's Meldebescheinigung and the last school report from your home country.
- Children who do not speak enough German first attend a Vorbereitungsklasse (preparation class) to learn German, with a teacher who supports them.
