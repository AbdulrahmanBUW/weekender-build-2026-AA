## Before you go
When you move to a new home in Dresden, you must register your address (Anmeldung) within **two weeks**. This is required by law. The service is **free of charge**.

You can register:
- **Online**, using an AusweisApp and an NFC-capable smartphone, or
- **In person** at a Bürgerbüro, with or without an appointment, or
- **Through another person**, if you give them a Vollmacht (power of attorney).

## What to bring
For in-person registration:
- **Personalausweis** or **Reisepass** (ID card or passport) for every person being registered
- **Formular Anmeldung** (registration form), filled out and signed. You can also get this form at the Bürgerbüro.
- **Wohnungsgeberbestätigung** (landlord confirmation). A rental contract is not enough. Even owners must submit this form. Your landlord is legally required to help with this.

For online registration:
- A Nutzerkonto (user account) with Online-Ausweis
- A valid ID card with an activated Online-Ausweisfunktion and PIN
- An NFC-capable smartphone or a USB card reader
- The AusweisApp
- The Wohnungsgeberbestätigung, if the home is not your own property

If someone else registers for you, they need a Vollmacht, the required documents, and your signed registration form.

If you have children under 16, the person whose home the child moves into must register the child.

## At the appointment
At the Bürgerbüro, your Anmeldung is processed right away. Staff will glue an address sticker into your ID card during the appointment.

If you register online, your registration is normally done within **3 working days**. You can then download your confirmation. The address sticker for your ID card is sent by post within **1 to 2 weeks**. You then stick it onto your ID yourself.

## Good to know
- If you have more than one home in Germany, the home you use most is your Hauptwohnung (main residence). A second home may lead to a Zweitwohnungssteuer (second-home tax).
- You have the right to object to your data being shared, for example with parties or address book publishers. Ask about an Übermittlungssperre (data block).
- After registering, tell the ARD ZDF Deutschlandradio Beitragsservice about your new address using their online contact form.
- You only need to deregister (Abmeldung) if you move abroad or give up your home without moving to a new one in Germany.
