Assumption-based; validate by interviewing 3–5 international attendees Saturday morning → log in `Runs/` as interview runs.

## A — Priya, international Master's student (MVP target)
- 24, from Pune, MSc Nanoelectronics at TU Dresden, 2nd semester, A1 German, TK public insurance, WG in Johannstadt.
- Phone-native; Google Translate + ChatGPT for letters; WhatsApp groups for tips; avoids calls.
- Needs Hausarzt + dermatologist; later Ausländerbehörde extension.
- Pains: practice lines busy during lectures; freezes when receptionist speaks fast; doesn't know *Überweisung*, *Versichertenkarte*; hung up twice.
- Quote: *"I can write a thesis in English, but I can't book a doctor."*

## B — Amina, family-reunion spouse (impact slide, v2)
- 31, Syria/Türkiye, A2 integration course, 3-year-old child, little English.
- Husband makes all calls in his breaks; she uses voice messages.
- Needs Kinderarzt (U-Untersuchungen), Frauenarzt.

## C — Marco, Blue Card engineer (first B2B buyer)
