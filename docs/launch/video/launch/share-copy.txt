Introducing Ankommen, your guide to starting in Germany: courses, events and help for families in Dresden, in six languages. When a call in German is needed, Call for me: an AI assistant makes the call in German, says it is an AI, and brings the answer back in your language (demo: calls are role-played in the browser, no real phone line yet). Built this weekend in Dresden with Lovable, n8n, Supabase, Deepgram, ElevenLabs and Claude: ankommen-dresden.lovable.app
