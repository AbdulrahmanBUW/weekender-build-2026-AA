Find it. We call for you.

Dresden mit Kind helps international parents find the right course, Kita, doctor or community for their child. HalloTermin then phones that place in German for them and brings the answer back in their language.

Two ideas, one family in Dresden

HalloTermin

Abdul

Each idea fills the other's gap

Dresden mit Kind alone

Maria finds a Russian-speaking music class for her 4-year-old. The page says "call for a trial lesson". She has to call in German, during work hours, and ask about a waiting list. This is where she stops.

HalloTermin adds an "Ask for me" button that makes that call.

HalloTermin alone

The assistant can call anyone, but the parent first has to know who to call and what to ask. Newcomers don't know which Kita, which course or which Kinderarzt takes new families.

Dresden mit Kind adds the curated directory and guides that answer "who" and "what".

Maria's Tuesday, end to end

08:40 · Discover

Maria opens the site in Russian, filters Courses: age 3–6, music, language Russian, district Neustadt. Two results. directory

08:42 · Understand

The provider page shows ages, prices and "trial lesson on request". A guide card explains how course registrations usually work. provider page

08:43 · Act

She taps "Ask for me": "Is there a free spot or a trial lesson for my 4-year-old on Tuesday or Thursday afternoon?" She approves the card. The assistant opens the call with:

"Guten Tag! Hier ist die KI-Assistentin von Maria Ivanova. Ich rufe für sie an und wollte fragen, ob es in Ihrem Musikkurs für Vierjährige noch einen Platz oder eine Probestunde gibt."

Good day, this is Maria Ivanova's AI assistant. I'm calling for her to ask whether your music course for four-year-olds has a free spot or a trial lesson.
08:46 · Result

«Пробное занятие: четверг, 16:30. Возьмите сменную обувь.»

Trial lesson Thursday 16:30, bring indoor shoes. Added to her calendar; the course page now shows "checked by phone today".
